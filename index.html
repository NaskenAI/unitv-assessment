Quick-fire MCQs (5 questions, 2 marks each)

Q1 — Which leaked passwords still work? 15 marks

Your 8 assigned accounts:

Each appears in leaked_plain.txt, but some leak entries are old passwords that no longer match. For each account, find its salt and hash in the shards and check whether the leaked password actually verifies (sha256(salt+leaked) == hash).

Q2 — Count the reuse 20 marks

Your target account:

This account's password is in the leak and verifies. Across all ten shards, how many accounts reuse that same password? Every account has a different salt, so two accounts with the same password do not have the same stored hash.

Q3 — Who broke in, and when? 25 marks

Your target account:

In access.log, your account has several successful logins — but only one of them is the attacker breaking in, after a long run of failed guesses; the rest are the user's normal logins. Process the log with code, put your account's events in time order, and identify the real break-in.

Q4 — Design a simple virus 15 marks

Write pseudocode for a simple virus. Clearly mark where each of the four phases happens — dormant, propagation, triggering, execution — and show the infection marker that stops it re-infecting a file. Then, in two or three sentences, explain how it spreads from one program to another. (You may use an AI assistant to help, as with the other questions.)
