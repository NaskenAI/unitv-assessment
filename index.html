Quick-fire MCQs (5 questions, 2 marks each)

Q1 — Breach verification 15 marks

Your 8 assigned accounts:

An old breach dump has resurfaced. For each of your 8 accounts the dump lists a password, but some are stale. An entry still works only if sha256(salt + leaked) equals the stored hash. Report (a) how many still work, and (b) the still-valid password of your FIRST assigned account.

Q2 — Password reuse (blast radius) 20 marks

Your target account:

For your assigned account, how many accounts across all ten shards use the SAME password? Every account has its own salt, so reuse can't be found by comparing stored hashes — re-hash the password under each account's salt. Include the target.

Q3 — Spot the intruder 25 marks

Your target account:

Your account appears several times in access.log. One success is an intruder who broke in after a burst of failed attempts; normal logins have no failures right before them. Report (a) the number of failed attempts and (b) the break-in timestamp.

Q4 — Design a virus (lifecycle) 15 marks

In pseudocode, design a simple file-infecting virus. Show all four phases — dormant, propagation, triggering, execution — and an infection marker it writes and checks so it never infects the same file twice. In 2–3 sentences, explain how it spreads.
